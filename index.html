What Makes Us Different

Obsessive about every detail

01

Altitude Selection

Only berries grown above 800m elevation qualify. The cold nights create tighter skins and more concentrated flavour compounds.

From branch to your door within 72 hours. Every stage is temperature-controlled to lock in polyphenol content and freshness.

Our Farming Practices

Growing the right way

No shortcuts. No compromises. Just good farming.

🌧️
Rainwater Only Irrigation

Our fields are fed entirely by Atlantic rainfall collected in natural reservoirs. We never draw from aquifers or mains supply.

🐝
On-Site Apiary Programme

Twelve hives of native black bees live on the farm year-round, pollinating at exactly the right moment and producing their own wildflower honey.

🌱
Cover Crop Rotation

Every third row rests under legume cover crops, fixing nitrogen and rebuilding soil structure without a single bag of synthetic fertiliser.

♻️
Zero-Waste Packaging

All punnets are moulded from pressed blueberry pulp — a byproduct of our juice pressing — compostable in a home bin within 12 weeks.
